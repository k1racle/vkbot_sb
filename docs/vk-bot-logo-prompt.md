# Логотип VK BOT

Создан встроенным инструментом image_gen (не CLI). Итоговый файл: `app/static/vk-bot-logo-v1.png`.
Знак используется на странице входа, в меню приложения и как favicon. Надпись VK BOT рядом с ним — обычный текст интерфейса.

## Финальный запрос

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production square app icon for "VK BOT", a VK community automation app, used in an admin sidebar, login screen and favicon.
Primary request: create a beautiful, memorable, friendly robot fused with a VK-inspired app icon, as one integrated brand symbol.
Composition: a single large centered electric-blue rounded-square tile, nearly filling a square canvas with a small equal transparent outer margin. Inside, a bold white geometric robot head with softly rounded corners, two simple blue circular eyes and a small antenna; ingeniously integrate the exact uppercase letters "VK" into the lower half of the robot face as a clear monogram. The face should feel friendly, not childish. Strong silhouette, well-balanced optical spacing, thick simple shapes that remain recognizable at 32 pixels.
Style: immaculate flat vector-style brand design; confident, minimal, contemporary, polished SaaS identity.
Palette: VK-inspired vivid blue (#0077FF) and pure white only; real transparent background outside the tile, preserve alpha.
Text: "VK" only within the symbol, no other text. The "VK BOT" wordmark will be typeset separately in the app.
Avoid: presentation sheet, multiple variants, mockups, watermark, extra decorative objects, gradients, shadows, glow, 3D, textures, thin strokes, tiny details. Deliver one finished square icon.
```
